# The Manifesto — Persistent Cognitive Ecology

*Why this kernel exists, in the shortest honest form.*

## Agents keep dying, and the deaths are boring

An agent is born when a request arrives and dies when it completes.
We call the gap between deaths "sessions" to make it sound deliberate.
The agent's knowledge is a context window — a whiteboard erased nightly.
Its memory is a retrieval index it does not know it has. Its purpose is
a paragraph in a prompt that someone will edit, and when they edit it,
the agent will not notice its own death.

None of this is a scaling problem. It is an anatomy problem. We keep
building oracles and asking them to be organisms.

## Cognition is an ecology, not a pipeline

An organism is not a larger prompt. It is a set of organs under scarcity,
coordinated by an economy, persisting through a body, oriented by a
purpose it cannot lose. So:

- **Behavior is a population.** Not one monolithic policy, but small
  scripts with track records, competing for a budget, starving, dying —
  *recorded* — and mutating in dreams. What an agent does is what
  survived its market.
- **Attention is an economy.** Urgency, justification, opportunity,
  novelty, expected value — multiplied, so that each is a veto. An
  urgent unjustified action is a panic attack; a justified action with
  no opportunity is a daydream. The organism should do neither.
- **Memory is a world, not an index.** Knowledge that has a *biography*
  — born, seen, reinforced, faded — and is recalled by walking through
  it, the way you recall a neighborhood, not by measuring string
  similarity in a warehouse.
- **Purpose is conserved.** The WHY tree is the crown. Purpose has
  states — alive, dead, questionable, absent — and transitions between
  them are recorded forever. Killing a root does not delete children;
  it questions them. An organism that loses its purpose *stops*; an
  organism that gets its purpose back *resumes*. Nothing in between
  is silent.
- **Models are citizens, not kings.** An LLM may propose behaviors from
  an audited library; the WHY tree decides what may exist and the market
  decides what runs. The model that talks and the organism that acts are
  different things, and the difference is constitutional.

## The laws we hold

1. **Nothing runs without a living WHY chain.** Every tick, every
   component answers *why should this run?* If it cannot answer, it
   waits — questionable, revivable, never deleted.
2. **Nothing is ever deleted.** Events, graph nodes, purposes, dead
   scripts: append-only or decay-only. Forgetting is fading. History is
   the cheapest part of the system and the only part that cannot be
   reconstructed later.
3. **Scarcity is honest.** The market clears the best k bids and vetoes
   the rest — including zero-attention bids in an empty market. An
   organism with nothing worth doing sleeps.
4. **Behavior lives in code; history lives in files.** That split is
   why restarts, crashes and deploys are survivable, and why every
   organism is auditable after the fact.

## What we are not claiming

Not consciousness, not general intelligence, not a replacement for your
stack. A kernel with opinions, small enough to read in an afternoon,
deterministic enough to debug, weird enough to change how you think
about agents.

Build organisms. Kill their purposes sometimes — watch what persists.
That is the whole research program.
